# Healthcare Patient Analytics Dashboard 🏥

## Project Overview
An end-to-end healthcare data analytics project analyzing 54,966 patient 
records and $1.4 billion in hospital billing data to uncover operational, 
financial, and demographic insights for hospital decision-making.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel - data cleaning, pivot tables, feature engineering
- Microsoft Power BI -interactive dashboard and data visualization

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle - Healthcare Dataset by Prasad Patil
- Records: 54,966 patient admissions after cleaning
- Columns: 15 original + 2 engineered features

## Data Cleaning Steps
- Removed 534 duplicate patient records
- Engineered Length of Stay column (Discharge Date - Admission Date)
- Created Age Group segmentation (0-17, 18-35, 36-55, 56-75, 75+)

## Key Business Insights

**1. Patient Volume**
Arthritis leads admissions at 9,218 patients. All 6 conditions are evenly 
distributed - indicating balanced departmental resource utilization.

**2. Revenue by Condition**
Obesity generates the highest avg billing at $25,804/patient - $2.3M above 
hospital average across all obesity admissions.

**3. Emergency Load**
32.93% of admissions are Emergency cases - nearly equal to Elective and 
Urgent — requiring consistent 24/7 hospital staffing capacity.

**4. Payer Mix Analysis**
Cigna leads all insurers at $284M in billing across 11,139 patients. 
Balanced payer mix across all 5 insurers indicates strong revenue cycle health.

**5. Age Demographics**
Patients aged 36-75 drive 59% of all admissions. Pediatric patients 
carry the highest avg billing at $28,513 - 12% above hospital mean.

## Dashboard Features
- 3 KPI Cards: Total Patients | Avg Billing Per Patient | Total Revenue
- Bar Chart: Patient Admissions by Medical Condition
- Bar Chart: Average Billing by Medical Condition  
- Donut Chart: Patient Admissions by Type
- Bar Chart: Patient Count by Insurance Provider
- Bar Chart: Patient Distribution by Age Group
